Smiling Happy Face

A smiling happy face typically features a round, cheerful expression. The eyes are often wide and bright, with eyebrows slightly raised to convey joy and warmth. The mouth curves into a broad, friendly smile, sometimes showing teeth or dimples, depending on the style. The overall vibe is light-hearted and positive, radiating happiness and friendliness, often depicted with vibrant colors like yellow to amplify the sense of joy.
