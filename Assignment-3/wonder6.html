Roman Colosseum, Italy

The Roman Colosseum in Rome, Italy, is one of the greatest architectural and engineering feats of ancient Rome. Completed in 80 AD, this massive amphitheater could hold up to 80,000 spectators and was used for gladiatorial contests, public spectacles, and other forms of entertainment. The Colosseum's grand design, with its arched entrances and tiered seating, reflects the Romans' ingenuity in architecture and public infrastructure. Despite the damage from earthquakes and stone robbers, it remains an enduring symbol of ancient Roman culture and power.
