Petra, Jordan

Petra, often called the "Rose City" because of the pink sandstone cliffs, is an ancient city carved into the rock in the deserts of Jordan. Built around the 4th century BC by the Nabataeans, it served as a major trading hub for caravans traveling through the region. Its most famous structure, the Treasury (Al-Khazneh), is an architectural marvel with intricate facades carved directly into the rock. Petra's blend of natural beauty and human craftsmanship makes it one of the most stunning archaeological sites in the world, offering a glimpse into the ancient world.
