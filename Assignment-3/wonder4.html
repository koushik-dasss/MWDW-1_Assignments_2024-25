Machu Picchu, Peru

Nestled in the Andes Mountains of Peru, Machu Picchu is a breathtaking relic of the Inca civilization, dating back to the 15th century. This "Lost City of the Incas" is renowned for its sophisticated dry-stone construction, terraced hillsides, and its ability to harmonize with the surrounding landscape. Abandoned in the 16th century during the Spanish Conquest, it remained hidden from the world until its rediscovery in 1911. Machu Picchu is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a symbol of the ingenuity and grandeur of the Inca Empire.
