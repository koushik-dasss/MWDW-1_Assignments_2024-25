Slightly Smiling Face

A slightly smiling face features a more subtle, gentle expression of happiness. The eyes are calm, often depicted as soft, relaxed, or neutral, with a slight upward curve of the lips forming a modest smile. The eyebrows are generally at rest, giving a serene and mild look, lacking the excitement of a broad grin. This face often conveys a sense of quiet contentment, friendliness, or politeness without being overly enthusiastic. The expression is understated yet pleasant, suggesting warmth and calmness in a minimal way.
