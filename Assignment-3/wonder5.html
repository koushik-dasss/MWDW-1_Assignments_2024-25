Chichen Itza, Mexico

Chichen Itza, located on the Yucatán Peninsula in Mexico, was one of the largest and most significant cities of the Maya civilization. The site's centerpiece is the Temple of Kukulkan, a pyramid known for its precise astronomical alignments, with a stairway that casts a shadow resembling a serpent during the equinoxes. Chichen Itza flourished between the 9th and 12th centuries and showcases the advanced knowledge of mathematics, astronomy, and engineering of the Maya people. The city was a religious, cultural, and political hub, and its ruins continue to captivate historians and tourists alike.
