Taj Mahal, India

The Taj Mahal is a stunning white marble mausoleum located in Agra, India, and is widely regarded as the epitome of Mughal architecture. Commissioned by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal, it was completed in 1643 after 22 years of construction. The Taj Mahal is famous for its symmetrical beauty, with its large dome, intricate carvings, and reflecting pools that enhance its majestic aura. As a symbol of eternal love, the Taj Mahal attracts millions of visitors each year, standing as a testament to both the artistic and emotional legacy of the Mughal Empire.
