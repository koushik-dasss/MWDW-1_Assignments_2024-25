Smiling Face

A smiling face features a bright and joyful expression. The eyes are typically wide and sparkling, often accompanied by slightly raised eyebrows to enhance the cheerful look. The mouth forms a broad, upward curve, revealing a full, friendly smile that sometimes shows teeth. The face radiates warmth, happiness, and friendliness, creating an inviting and positive vibe. This expression exudes a sense of contentment, excitement, or genuine joy, often depicted with glowing or vibrant colors like yellow to emphasize the upbeat mood.
