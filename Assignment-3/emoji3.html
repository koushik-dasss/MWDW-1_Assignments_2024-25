Sad Face

A sad face is characterized by a downturned expression that conveys sorrow or disappointment. The eyes may appear soft, half-closed, or slightly droopy, sometimes with a hint of tears or a glossy look to suggest sadness. The eyebrows are typically slanted upward in the middle, creating a worried or hurt appearance. The mouth forms a downward curve, reflecting the feeling of unhappiness or distress. Overall, the face exudes a sense of melancholy or emotional pain, often depicted in muted colors or with minimal detail to emphasize the subdued mood.
