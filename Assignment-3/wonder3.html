Christ the Redeemer, Brazil

Towering over Rio de Janeiro from the peak of Mount Corcovado, the Christ the Redeemer statue is one of the most iconic symbols of Brazil and Christianity. Completed in 1931, the 98-foot tall statue, with its outstretched arms, overlooks the city and represents peace and the open-heartedness of the Brazilian people. Crafted from reinforced concrete and soapstone, it took nine years to complete. The statue is a beacon of hope, attracting millions of visitors and standing as a testament to the unity of faith and humanity.
